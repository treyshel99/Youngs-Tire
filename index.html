Our History

About Young's Tire Store

Founded in 1966, Young's Tire Store, Inc. is Pontotoc, Mississippi's leading tire, wheel, and automotive repair shop — family-owned and operated by Cal Young, carrying forward a tradition of quality workmanship that's now spanned generations.

We're proud to have been voted Best Tire Dealer by the people of Pontotoc County, and we hold certifications from AAA, TIA, ASA, and the BBB. It's the same simple approach that's kept customers coming back since 1966: fair pricing, honest work, and a shop that treats you like a neighbor — because you are one.
